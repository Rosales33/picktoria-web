# Delete your Picktoria account

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

Picktoria is an app by Pablo Rosales Rodríguez. You can delete your account in two ways:

## From the app

**Menu → Account settings → Delete my account**, and confirm it. It's immediate.

## Without opening the app

Write to **[privacidad@picktoria.es](mailto:privacidad@picktoria.es)** from the email address you signed up with, with the subject
"Delete my account". We delete it within one month at most and reply when it's done. If you write
from another address, we'll ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

## What happens to your data

- **Your account is anonymised on the spot**: your email and your display name disappear, you
  leave all your leagues and your sign-in account is deleted.
- **Your picks** stay in their leagues signed as "Deleted user", so that everyone else's standings
  still add up.
- **Your comments** stop being visible on the spot; we keep them hidden for up to a year in case a
  complaint about them needs dealing with, and then they're deleted.
- **Anything you bought**: the record of the purchase stays for six years, without your email or
  your name, because the law requires us to keep it. The premium you paid for stays with its
  league.
- **What you wrote to Support** is deleted one year after you wrote it.

There's no subscription to cancel: premium doesn't renew by itself.

All the details are in the [privacy policy](https://picktoria.es/en/privacy).
