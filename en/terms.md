## 2. What Picktoria is

Picktoria is a **social sports prediction game**: you create private leagues with your friends,
make picks on real matches and races, and earn points when you get them right.

**No money is played for in Picktoria.** Points **have no monetary value**: they can't be bought,
sold, exchanged for money or for prizes, or passed on to another person. They only serve to order
the standings of your leagues. **Picktoria offers no prizes** of any kind.

Picktoria **is not affiliated with or sponsored by** any league, competition, club, team, driver
or federation. Their names and trademarks belong to their owners, and appear only to identify the
matches.

## 4. How the game works

The rules are explained in the app. The essentials:

- **Each pick costs points**, and if you get it right you receive more according to its
  multiplier. Your points in a league can go below zero.
- **Multipliers are calculated by Picktoria** from match data, and they can change until you
  confirm a pick: the pick keeps the multiplier you saw when you confirmed it.
- **Picks are resolved with the real results**, which reach us from a sports data provider. If a
  result reaches us wrong and we resolve it wrong, **we can correct it**, points included.
- **If a match is postponed or cancelled**, its pick is cancelled and we give back what it cost.
  In a combo, that selection is cancelled and you can replace it or leave the combo as it is.
- **If a player doesn't get to play**, whatever depended on them is cancelled, just like a
  postponement.

Picktoria doesn't guarantee that sports data will always arrive on time or without errors,
although we try to make it so.

## 5. Leagues

- **They're private**: you join with an invite code shared by their admin.
- **The admin** sets the league up, decides who stays —they can ban a member and readmit them— and
  can delete it. The duration and the week start can't be changed once it's created.
- **When it ends**, only its admin can see it, and they can renew it for a new season keeping its
  history, during the following 15 days.
- **A finished league is deleted one year** after it ends, with everything in it.

## 6. Community rules

Picktoria is played among friends, and some things aren't allowed in any comment, display name or
league name:

- insulting, harassing or threatening anyone;
- sexual, violent, discriminatory or illegal content;
- posting someone else's personal data;
- pretending to be someone else;
- spam and advertising.

**How they're enforced**: a word filter sets aside the most obvious; anyone can **report** a
comment or a person —without anyone knowing who reported— and **block** another person to stop
seeing what they write. Each league's admin decides about its members. And above that, Picktoria
can **remove content**, **change an offensive name** and **delete an account** that breaks these
rules: what it had written stops being visible, and its picks stay in their leagues as "Deleted
user" so that everyone else's standings still add up. The decision is always made by a person; the
filter only sets text aside.

**If you see something illegal** in Picktoria, even if you're not in that league, tell us with
Report or by writing to [soporte@picktoria.es](mailto:soporte@picktoria.es): we'll look into it and get back to you.

What you write is yours. You give us permission to store it and show it within its leagues, which
is what's needed to provide the service, for as long as it exists.

## 7. Premium

- **Premium belongs to a league**, not to a person: it gives all its members more competitions,
  more picks per week and longer combos. Exactly what it includes is explained in the app before
  you pay.
- **It's paid once for the league's duration** —3, 6 or 12 months— and lasts as long as the
  league does. **It doesn't renew by itself** and has no recurring charges.
- **The price is the one the store shows** when you pay, taxes included.
- **Apple or Google collects it**, and their purchase terms apply too.
- **Refunds are requested from the store**, under its policies. If the store gives the money back,
  the league goes back to free: it keeps the picks already made, and if it had more competitions
  than the free version allows, whoever asked for the refund chooses which ones to keep.
- **When you pay, premium is activated straight away**, and with that **you lose the right of
  withdrawal** for distance purchases: you lose it because you ask for it to start straight away
  (Art. 103(m) of the Spanish consumer protection act, the Ley General para la Defensa de los
  Consumidores y Usuarios). Even so, the store may give you your money back under its own terms,
  and then the league goes back to free, as the previous point says.
