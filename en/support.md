# Help and contact

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

## Write to us

- **From the app**: **Menu → Support**. It's the fastest way, because we know which account you're
  writing from.
- **By email**: **[soporte@picktoria.es](mailto:soporte@picktoria.es)**.

We reply as soon as we can, in English or in Spanish.

## Your data and your account

- For anything about your data: **[privacidad@picktoria.es](mailto:privacidad@picktoria.es)**. What we do with it is in the
  [privacy policy](https://picktoria.es/en/privacy).
- To delete your account: [how to delete it](https://picktoria.es/en/delete-account).

## Payments and refunds

Premium for a league is collected by the store, so **refunds are requested from the store**:

- **iPhone**: at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Android**: from Google Play, in your order history
  ([how to request one](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637)).

If the store gives you your money back, the league goes back to free and keeps the picks already
made.

## Something that shouldn't be there

If you see something illegal or offensive in Picktoria, report it from the app or write to us at
[soporte@picktoria.es](mailto:soporte@picktoria.es).
