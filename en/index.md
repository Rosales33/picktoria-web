# Compete with your friends by predicting sports results

Picktoria is an app for playing in a group. You create a private league, every week you make your
picks on real sports events —football, basketball and Formula 1 for now, with more to come— and
whoever gets the most right wins. Points are worth no money: what matters is finishing ahead.

**Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.**
