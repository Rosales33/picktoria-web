# Picktoria Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

_This is a translation of the [Spanish original](https://picktoria.es/privacidad). If the two ever
say different things, the one more favourable to you applies._

Picktoria is an app for competing with your friends by predicting sports results, in private
leagues and with points that are worth no money. This policy explains which of your data we
process, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights.

**In short:** we keep the minimum you need to play. Nobody but you ever sees your email address.
Everything lives on servers in the European Union, wherever you are. There is no advertising, no
analytics and no profiling, and we don't sell your data to anyone. If you delete your account, it
is anonymised on the spot.

## 2. What data we process and why

### Your account

To create it, sign in, verify your email and recover access: **your email address**, **your
password**, the **display name** you choose, whether your email is verified, the dates you joined
and left, and your notification preferences.

Your password is handled by our account provider, Amazon Cognito, which stores it encrypted and
checks it without it ever reaching us: **we never see it**. Inside your leagues the others only
see your display name; **we never show your email address to anyone**.

### The game

To provide the service: the leagues you belong to, with your role and your points; your picks and
how they were resolved; your comments and reactions; and the history of the seasons that are
renewed. **Only the members of each league see this**, and leagues are private: you join with an
invite code. The others can't see a pick of yours until its match starts.

### Keeping things friendly

So that groups work, and to meet the rules of the app stores:

- **A word filter** checks comments, display names and league names. It only rejects or sets aside
  text; it doesn't decide anything about you.
- **Reports**: who reports, whom, in which league and for what reason, from a fixed list. **We
  never tell anyone who reported**: we keep it only so that one person can't inflate the count.
- **Blocks**: who blocks whom. The person you block isn't told.
- **If someone breaks the rules**, we can remove what they wrote, change their display name or
  delete their account, and before deleting it we write to them with the reason. Whatever is
  removed stops being visible to everyone, but we keep it for a while —like what the filter sets
  aside— in case it's needed, for example to deal with a complaint (§5).

### Notifications

If you allow the app to send you notifications: your device's identifier for notifications, its
system (iOS or Android) and its language. Notifications may include the name of a league or the
display name of another person. **We don't store the text of notifications.** Each type of
notification can be turned off in Settings.

### Premium purchases

If you buy premium for a league: which league and which duration, the amount and the currency, the
status of the purchase, the store's identifier for the transaction and the dates of confirmation
or refund. **Payment is collected by Apple or Google**, who know who pays; **we never see your
card details**.

### Support

If you write to us from Support or by email: the type of request, your message and the email
address where you want our reply, which may not be your account's.

### Service security

To detect abuse and errors, and to be able to restore the service if something fails:

- **A log of every request to our servers**, with your **IP address**, your internal identifier,
  what was requested, the result and the app or browser that made it.
- **Server error logs**, with internal identifiers.
- **Backups** of the database.

### App errors

To find out what breaks on your phone: the error trace, your device's model and system, and the
app version. **Without your name, your email or your IP.**

### App updates

So that fixes reach you without waiting for a new version in the store: when it opens, the app
checks whether there's an update. That request carries your **IP address**, your system, the app
version and a random identifier for that installation. **Not your account or your email.**

### What we don't do

We don't use your data for **advertising**, we don't measure what you do in the app with
**analytics** tools, we don't build **profiles** and we don't make **automated decisions** about
you. Multipliers are calculated from the matches, not from people. **We don't sell or hand over
your data.**

## 3. Why we're allowed to process it

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Your account, the game, purchases and support | Performance of the contract you accept when you sign up, the terms (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Keeping a record of what was sold | A legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| Notifications | Your consent, which you give through the system permission and can withdraw whenever you like (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Keeping things friendly, security and app errors | Our legitimate interest in a safe service and a community that works (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

## 4. Who we share it with

With the **providers** we need to provide the service, who process it on our behalf and only for
that:

| Provider | What for | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon Web Services | The servers, the database, accounts (Cognito), logs and backups | Ireland (EU) |
| Expo | Sending notifications to your phone, and app updates | USA |
| Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Delivering notifications on Android | USA and elsewhere |
| Apple (Apple Push Notification service) | Delivering notifications on iPhone | USA and elsewhere |
| RevenueCat | Checking purchases made in the store; it receives a random identifier for your account, not your email | USA |
| Sentry | App errors | Germany (EU) |
| Zoho | The mailbox where we receive what you write to us | European Union |
| GitHub | Hosting the picktoria.es website, where this policy and the terms are. It logs the IP address of visitors, for its own security | USA |

**Apple and Google** collect payments as independent controllers, with their own privacy policies.

**Outside the EU**: when a provider is in the United States, the transfer relies on the **EU-US
Data Privacy Framework**, which Expo and GitHub have joined, or on the European Commission's
**standard contractual clauses**, which are part of our contracts with Google and RevenueCat.
Apple and Google deliver notifications as part of your phone's systems, with their own safeguards.

**The other members of your leagues** see your display name and what you do in them, as explained
in §2. Beyond that, we don't share your data with anyone unless the law requires us to.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Your account | Until you delete it. It is then **anonymised on the spot**: your email and your name are replaced with meaningless values, you leave all your leagues, your notification identifiers are deleted and so is your Amazon Cognito account, which held your real email and your password |
| Your picks, if you delete your account | They stay in their leagues signed as "Deleted user", so that everyone else's standings still add up |
| Your comments, if you delete your account | They stop being visible on the spot. We keep them hidden **for up to a year**, in case a complaint about them needs dealing with, and then they're deleted |
| What you wrote, if we delete your account for breaking the rules | It stops being visible on the spot, and we keep it hidden **for up to a year**. Your picks stay as "Deleted user" |
| Comments removed, or set aside by the filter | Hidden, **for up to a year** |
| A finished league | One year from when it ends, and then it's deleted with everything in it. Its admin can delete it sooner |
| Purchases | **Six years** from the purchase, which is how long the law requires a business to keep its records. If you delete your account, the record of the purchase stays without your email or your name, at RevenueCat too |
| Messages to Support | **One year** from when you write to us, in the database and in our mailbox |
| Your notification identifier | Until you sign out on that device or delete your account |
| The request log, with the IP, and the server errors | 30 days |
| Backups | 7 days |
| App errors | 30 days, which is how long Sentry keeps them on our plan |

## 6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to **access** your data, **rectify** it, **erase** it, **object** to its
processing or **restrict** it, and to take it to another service (**portability**).

- **Deleting your account** is something you can do yourself from Settings, and it's immediate. If
  you can't open the app, [picktoria.es/en/delete-account](https://picktoria.es/en/delete-account)
  explains how to ask us.
- **Your display name** can be changed in Settings. To change your email address, write to us.
- **Notifications** can be turned off in Settings or in your phone's permissions; doing so
  withdraws your consent.
- **A copy of your data**: we send it to you in a format that can be read and reused.
- **For everything else**, write to [privacidad@picktoria.es](mailto:privacidad@picktoria.es) —ideally from your account's email
  address, so that we know it's you— or from Support. We reply within one month at most.

If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the **Spanish Data
Protection Agency** ([www.aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) or, if you live in another country, to
the data protection authority there.

## 7. Minors

Picktoria is only for people **aged 18 or over**, and you confirm it when you sign up. If we learn
that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it, and what they had written stops being visible.

## 8. How we protect your data

- All connections are **encrypted**, including those between our servers.
- The database is **encrypted** and sits in an **isolated network** that can't be reached from the
  internet.
- Your password **doesn't pass through our servers**, and your session is stored **encrypted** on
  your phone.
- Each part of the system has **only the permissions it needs**, and everything done in the
  infrastructure account is **logged** and monitored.

## 9. Cookies

**Neither the app nor the website uses cookies, analytics or tracking technologies.** The website
is a static page; it's hosted by GitHub, which logs the IP address of visitors for its own
security (§4).

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change it in a way that affects you, we'll let you know in the app before it applies. The
date of the latest version is at the top.
